Privacy Policy

1. Controller

KG Models SIA
Valnu Iela 5-6, Riga, LV-1050, Latvia
Email: lauris.kalnins@wundermgmt.com

Personal data is processed in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Latvian data protection law.

2. What this website collects

This website is a static site. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

When you visit, our hosting provider (Render Services, Inc.) automatically records standard server log data: your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, and the date and time of access. This is technically necessary to deliver the website and to protect it against attacks (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest). Log data is not combined with any other data and is deleted routinely.

3. When you contact or apply to us

If you email us, we store your email address and the contents of your message to handle your request (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not pass this data to third parties.

If you book a call through our application link, the booking is handled by Calendly LLC on their own pages, under Calendly's privacy policy. We receive the details you enter there (such as your name, email, and answers to the application questions) and use them only to prepare and hold the call.

4. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as long as the law requires, and then delete it.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can withdraw any consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email lauris.kalnins@wundermgmt.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Latvia, this is the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija), www.dvi.gov.lv.

6. Embedded videos and external links

The results page contains YouTube videos in privacy-enhanced mode. No data is sent to YouTube when you load the page — a connection to YouTube (Google Ireland Ltd.) is made only if you click play, at which point Google's privacy policy applies.

This website also links to external services (for example Calendly). Once you leave our site, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.
